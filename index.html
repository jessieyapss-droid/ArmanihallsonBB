07Before you ask

What people ask first.

What's the price?

It depends on layout, floor and facing, and packages are updated from time to time. WhatsApp me and I'll send you the current figure for the layouts you're interested in.

What is the dual-key Type A1?

Type A1 (642 sq ft) is split into two separate halves, each with its own bathroom, sharing one foyer. Live in one side and rent out the other, or rent both separately — a strong angle for investors.

Is it really freehold?

Yes. The land tenure is freehold.

How is public transport?

It's a transit-oriented development with MRT access. At the viewing I'll show you the exact station and walking route.

How much is the monthly maintenance?

RM0.98 psf. A 583 sq ft unit works out to roughly RM571 a month. Every layout above has its figure worked out.

Does it come with parking?

The development has 638 parking lots across levels 1 to 10. Allocation differs by unit type — I'll take you through exactly what applies to your layout at the viewing.

Do I have to pay or commit to anything to view?

No. The viewing is free and carries no obligation. Just bring your questions.

I'm outstation or overseas. Can I still view?

Yes. WhatsApp me and we can arrange a video walkthrough, with the material sent to you directly.
